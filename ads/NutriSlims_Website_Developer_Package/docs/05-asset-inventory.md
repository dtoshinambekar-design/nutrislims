## Important asset notes
- Photos were AI-generated for this design handoff. They do not depict the real clinician, clinic, staff or patients.
- Obtain actual clinic/clinician photos with permission before production.
- Use the original logo file from the owner. Do not treat the temporary SVG as the official mark.
- Provide alt text based on actual final image content.
- Confirm image usage rights and privacy before using any third-party imagery.
