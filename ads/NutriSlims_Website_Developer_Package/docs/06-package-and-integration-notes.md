# Package and integration notes

## Supplied video links
The video manifest includes all original user-supplied Drive URLs and embed URLs. Video binaries are not bundled: they remain in the user's Google Drive. This avoids creating copies without confirming permission and avoids a misleading claim that the videos are included locally.

## Prototype scope
The static prototype demonstrates responsive layout, mobile navigation, BMI calculation, content sections and the three supplied video links. Booking submission, Google review retrieval, newsletter subscription, recipe persistence and the client portal require backend/service configuration and are intentionally not represented as completed integrations.

## Before public launch
- Confirm Drive link access in an incognito session.
- Replace placeholder images and logo with owner-approved files.
- Verify clinic contact data and opening hours.
- Approve credentials, reviews, patient stories and clinical claims.
- Connect booking, CRM, newsletter, authentication and CMS services.
