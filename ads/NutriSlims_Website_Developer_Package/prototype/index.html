EVIDENCE-BASED HEALTH TOOLS

Interactive Health Calculators Hub

Educational assessment tools to help you understand your baseline body metrics and nutritional parameters.

Advanced Body Mass Index (BMI)

Calculate your BMI using standard WHO and Asian-Indian screening cutoffs.

Asian-Indian BMI Cut-offs:

  • Underweight: < 18.5
  • Normal Weight: 18.5 – 22.9
  • Overweight: 23.0 – 24.9
  • Obesity: ≥ 25.0

Formula: BMI = Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]².
Note: BMI does not differentiate between muscle mass and fat tissue. Consult our clinic for bio-impedance body composition analysis.

BMR (Harris-Benedict Equation)

Estimate your Basal Metabolic Rate — the baseline calories your body burns at complete rest.

Understanding Your BMR

Your BMR accounts for 60–75% of your total daily caloric expenditure, powering your heart, lungs, brain, and cellular repair.

Revised Harris-Benedict Formula:
• Men: 88.362 + (13.397 × kg) + (4.799 × cm) - (5.677 × age)
• Women: 447.593 + (9.247 × kg) + (3.098 × cm) - (4.330 × age)

Daily Water Intake Estimator

Calculate your recommended daily fluid intake based on body weight and activity level.

Hydration & Metabolism

Adequate hydration is critical for digestive motility, kidney filtration, nutrient absorption, and hormonal regulation.

Clinical Benchmark: Approx. 35ml per kg of body weight + 350–500ml for every 30 minutes of strenuous exercise or high ambient heat.

Waist-to-Hip Ratio (WHR)

Assess abdominal fat distribution and cardiovascular risk markers.

WHO Cardiometabolic Risk Categories

Women: Low: < 0.80 | Moderate: 0.81 – 0.85 | High: > 0.85

Men: Low: < 0.90 | Moderate: 0.91 – 0.99 | High: ≥ 1.00

WHR = Waist (cm) / Hip (cm). Abdominal visceral fat is strongly linked to insulin resistance, metabolic syndrome, and PCOS symptoms.

Ideal Body Weight (Devine Formula)

Calculate estimated ideal body weight based on height and biological sex.

Clinical Application

The Devine formula is widely used in clinical pharmacology and nutrition to establish baseline metabolic reference weight.

Reference:
• Men: 50 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 feet.
• Women: 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 feet.
