## Content approval checklist
- [ ] Clinic name/address/phone/email/hours confirmed.
- [ ] Dietitian's exact credentials confirmed against certificates.
- [ ] Current services and delivery modes confirmed.
- [ ] No unsupported cure, reversal, guarantee or outcome claims.
- [ ] Patient stories and identifiable photos have written consent.
- [ ] Google review source and exact listing confirmed.
- [ ] Review counts and clinic statistics are current and date-stamped.
- [ ] Video content reviewed; titles, descriptions and duration are accurate.
- [ ] Recipes, nutrition calculations and health labels reviewed by qualified professional.
- [ ] All health tools have formula and disclaimer sign-off.

## Content governance
Use CMS fields for `status` (draft/review/approved/published), `reviewedBy`, `reviewedAt`, `source`, `consentRef` and `expiryDate` where applicable. Health content should be reviewed periodically and when clinical guidance changes.
